# R8: Submission & Assembly: evaluation

## What the brief asks
Submit as:
1. **Google Doc / PDF** with Tasks 1, 3, 4, 6, 7
2. **Live Prototypes** (Task 2): A, B and C, each with its own URL (D-10)
3. **Financial Model** (Task 5)

"Deadline: 72 Hours. Late submissions will not be reviewed." Shortlisted candidates go to a **60-minute live working session**.

## What reviewers will actually judge
- **Completeness.** One missing deliverable caps the verdict.
- **Skimmability.** The founder gets the decisions from headings and TL;DRs in about 10 minutes.
- **Links work** for someone with no access to your accounts.
- **Consistency** across Tasks 1, 3, 4, 6 and all three prototypes.
- **No signs of unedited AI output:** filler, hedging, invented statistics.

## Ambiguities and how we resolve them
| Ambiguity | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Doc or PDF? | Both: the Google Doc as primary, the PDF as backup (A16) |
| Should Task 2 and 5 write-ups also appear in the doc? | Yes, a short section with the links + TL;DR, so the doc is the single entry point. Task 2 gets three sub-sections (A, B, C), each with its own write-up. |
| Prototype B shows features R4 cuts or defers (switchboard tiles) | Point to R4's prototype-vs-production note in the Task 2 and Task 4 sections |
| Deadline time | Submit as soon as R8 passes, inside 72 h; quality first (D-07) |

## Risks
| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Permission errors ("request access") | Incognito test of every link |
| Rushed or fatigued final hour | QA (R8) is a hard gate; take a break before assembly |
| Contradictions between tasks | Consistency checklist |

## Effort and priority
- **Estimate:** 1 h · **Priority:** P0 gate · **Depends on:** R1–R7
