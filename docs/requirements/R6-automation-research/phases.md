| Phase | Goal | Entry | Activities | Exit | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A1. Design flow** | End-to-end lead flow | R3 triggers | Tools, steps, data contract | Every step has an owner and data fields | 15 m |

| **B2. Verify** | Nothing unsourced | Gathered facts | Open each source; date it | All numbers verified or removed | 8 m |
| **B3. Decide** | Clear recommendation | Verified facts | Cities, risk, go/no-go with conditions | One-line recommendation + kill criteria | 10 m |
